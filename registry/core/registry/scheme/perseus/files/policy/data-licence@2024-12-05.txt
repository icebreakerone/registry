# Use of Perseus consent text in Scheme data transfers

All data transferred between participants must use a licence where an immutable definitive version is stored in the Registry as an RDF resource (‘Licence Resource’).

Each Licence Resource has a URL where the Licence Resource can be retrieved and acts as the identifier in Provenance records.

Where the Licence Resource in the Registry includes an `ib1:permissionText` term, the party (Perseus member) providing the data must:
1. have obtained permission using the wording in that term, and
2. assert that they can provide evidence of consent by including a Permission step within the Provenance record, and including the `id` of this step in the `permissions` property of the Transfer step.
