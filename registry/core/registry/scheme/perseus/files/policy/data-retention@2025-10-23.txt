# Data Retention Policy for Perseus
Version: v2025-10-23

This policy provides information for Carbon Accounting Provider and Financial Service Provider members of the Perseus Scheme. Scheme policies do not apply to Energy Data Provider data retention and deletion. Any queries can be directed to perseus@ib1.org. 

Where not defined in this policy, capitalised terms have the meaning given to them in the Scheme Agreement.

## Deletion Date
The Deletion Date is six (6) months after data transfer.

## Data retention
Participants should retain Consumption Data for the minimum viable period required to execute their roles and responsibilities within this Scheme. The Carbon Accounting Provider must delete Consumption Data at the latest by its Deletion Date. The Financial Service Provider will retain Derivative Data in line with statutory and fund requirements.

This authorisation may be extended by a party with proportionate legal ground to process the data, subject to Participant permission, to continue to retain data for a defined period related to a specific purpose.
